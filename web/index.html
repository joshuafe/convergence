Solo lab
AROUND THE TABLE · 2–4 PLAYERS

Talk your way
to the connection.

  1. Read your private perspective. Three words point to an idea. Several words on the board might fit. Nobody is given the answer.
  2. Invent a clue. Write a word or short phrase inspired by your evidence. Tap 1–3 board words it might connect to, then post. Everyone’s clues appear together.
  3. Compare and discuss. Find the one board word that fits all the perspectives. Edit your clue, change your attached words, or share your original evidence whenever you like.
  4. Agree together. Propose a word. Every player must confirm before it is checked. A wrong answer simply reopens discussion.

You can read everything aloud. Try your own clues first for the fun of it. There is no timer, penalty, or rule against talking.

Use a nickname. Rooms last 24 hours. Keep the room code within your group. A refreshed tab resumes your player; each player needs their own device or separate browser session.
